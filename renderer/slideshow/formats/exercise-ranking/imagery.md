# exercise-ranking Imagery

## Scope

This file owns the reusable visual interpretation of the designated `slideshow/exercise-ranking` references. It consumes `context/imagery.md` and does not own copy, exercise claims, project coordinates, provider configuration, or production assets.

The designated source evidence is the ordered eight-image post `7653231448740154644` under `references/`. The reference media is execution evidence only and is not a production asset.

## Visual grammar

- Use full-bleed vertical candid gym or personal-record photography rather than white exercise cards or anatomy-led diagrams.
- Give each slide one immediately recognizable subject: the relevant body-region context, exercise setup, or exercise-in-use view.
- Overlay one compact centered text stack with strong contrast. Preserve enough local negative space that the position, exercise name, and rationale remain readable without covering the decisive body or equipment detail.
- Use a stable typographic treatment across the sequence, but derive exact text position from each image's safe negative space rather than copying source coordinates.
- Keep essential text away from the right-side TikTok controls and lower interface zone.

## Capture carriers

The source alternates personal POV, mirror/self-record, seated equipment POV, and equipment-detail views. Preserve that personal-record function through a heterogeneous set rather than one coordinated campaign shoot.

- Prefer plausible self-shots or openly shared friend/training-partner angles with ordinary gym light, imperfect framing, equipment clutter, and non-performative posture.
- Make it easy to answer who held the camera and why the image exists.
- When the exercise must be identified precisely, show enough machine, bench, limb position, grip, and body orientation to distinguish the intended setup.
- Do not use a body-only crop as proof of an exercise outcome.
- Reject voyeuristic angles, hidden-observer framing, commercial physique posing, polished advertisement lighting, synthetic anatomy, and impossible camera causality.

## Sequence roles

- **Cover:** a direct body-region or equipment-context image that supports the comparison promise without implying transformation proof.
- **Item slides:** one exercise-specific candid image per ranked item, with enough visual evidence to identify the setup.
- **Optional context:** only a truthful first-party context image when separately supported; never adapt the reference's physique comparison as testimony.
- **Product insert:** exclude the source app screen during prelaunch. It is source-specific promotion, not reusable visual grammar.

## Cross-slide consistency

- Keep text contrast, hierarchy, and mobile-safe margins consistent while allowing exposure, camera height, and carrier type to vary naturally.
- Avoid repeating one cast, location, lens distance, pose, or equipment angle across the full sequence.
- Inspect one ordered contact sheet first, then full-resolution slides only where text, setup, or crop remains ambiguous.
- Remove or exclude TikTok UI, source branding, watermarks, and unrelated product screens from any future adaptation.
