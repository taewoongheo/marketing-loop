# Imagery: livegoodphysique

## Scope

This file owns the reusable visual interpretation of the designated `livegoodphysique` references. It consumes the account-wide rules in `context/imagery.md`, the selected approved copy, and the ordered raw evidence under `references/`. It does not own project coordinates, content-specific scenes, provider configuration, or final image bytes.

## Reference-derived visual grammar

- Treat each slide as an independent found-camera moment selected for that item's role. The reference family deliberately mixes mirror selfies, extreme limb or exercise-detail crops, point-of-view training shots, loaded equipment, bench-and-bottle still lifes, empty Gym structures, food or recovery objects, and occasional offbeat visual metaphors.
- A muscular adult man may anchor the cover or a body-relevant item, but do not require a person on every slide. Across one list, vary among one or two people-led images, close physical details, equipment or Gym-environment still lifes, and a copy-relevant object or metaphor when supported by the references.
- When a person appears, prefer real sourced photography and the references' ordinary mirror, POV, cropped-body, or companion-shot viewpoints. For a companion shot, use the reference family's open frontal or three-quarter line of sight from within the same bench, rack, or dumbbell station; the subject may glance at the camera or remain focused while plainly sharing the space with the photographer. Equipment may frame an edge but must not conceal the photographer behind rack uprights or create a covert-observer viewpoint. Do not manufacture staged three-quarter poses, coordinated facial expressions, repeated seated-rest poses, or a consistent cast and location merely to make the slideshow look designed.
- Preserve heterogeneous phone-camera evidence: mixed exposure and color temperature, incidental reflections, imperfect tilt, hard flash or flat practical light, low-resolution texture, arbitrary edge crops, clutter, and ordinary surfaces. Do not normalize every slide into dark cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, polished skin, controlled negative space, or one repeated Gym palette.
- Let object-led slides remain object-led. Plates, a bar sleeve, bench, straps, bottle, towel, shoes, notebook, food, an empty rack, or another truthful training-adjacent object may dominate without adding a decorative lifter.
- Copy the references' slide-role sequence and capture type, not their media. Do not reproduce a reference person's identity, exact body, pose, photograph, embedded wording, app interface, child or meme subject, branding, or distinctive composition.
- Do not use exercise GIFs, anatomical illustrations, isolated exercise diagrams, motion-phase composites, or their extracted still frames.
- Centered outlined copy may sit directly over busy photography as in the references; preserve decisive body or equipment details, but do not invent studio-like empty space solely to accommodate text.
