This file owns how a selected LIFT CODE message is expressed through language in the `never-giveup-109` slideshow format: the bounded tier-list premise, ranked item wording, compact judgment, optional non-ranked interstitials, post title, and caption. It does not own exercise truth, exact slide count, photography, geometry, typography, or image generation.

- Product and launch boundaries come from `context/product.md` and `docs/marketing-funnel.md`.
- Reusable training claims and limitations come from accepted `expertise_entries` retrieved through `context/expertise.md`.
- `context/imagery.md` owns account-wide visual direction; this format's `imagery.md` owns the reusable visual interpretation of its designated references.
- The designated raw posts under `references/post1/` and `references/post2/` are execution evidence only. Do not copy their wording, exercise choices, ratings, physiques, photographs, branding, product interface, or distinctive expressions.
- Search bars, navigation arrows, page dots, black side surrounds, source controls, and repeated acquisition captures are viewer chrome rather than format grammar.

The references use a blunt tier-list premise, descending numbered items, one short rating, and one compact reason. They also contain aspirational or product-promotion interstitials. During LIFT CODE's current prelaunch phase, replace product promotion with standalone decision value and do not mention the app or planned capabilities.

## Progression

Use a cover, a descending ranked sequence, and only the non-ranked interstitials needed to preserve the reference family's rhythm or clarify the decision. The number of slides follows the approved content, not a reusable template.

### Cover

State a narrow ranking or tier-list premise that can be understood without the caption.

```text
[specific decision] tier list
```

or

```text
Top [number] [options or checks]
for [specific training stake]
```

- Keep the premise compact and immediately recognizable to a lifter.
- Rank only items that share one coherent decision basis.
- Do not promise rapid physique or performance outcomes.

### Ranked item

Each ranked slide uses three language roles in this order:

```text
[number]. [item]
[rating]
[one short practical judgment]
```

- Descend toward the strongest or most decision-relevant item.
- Treat the rating as a clearly subjective practical judgment, not scientific precision.
- Keep the reason to one compact sentence or two short lines.
- Preserve a material non-equivalence or scope boundary when omitting it would mislead.
- The final item should resolve the premise with the clearest decision rule.

### Non-ranked interstitial

A non-ranked slide may provide one aspirational stake, visual pause, or practical rule when it strengthens the sequence.

```text
[short decision principle]
[one-line consequence or action]
```

- Do not add filler solely to match a reference slide count.
- Do not insert an app reveal, fake interface, or product CTA during prelaunch.
- A practical-rule interstitial must follow from accepted evidence already used by the ranked sequence.

## Voice and density

- Use direct, idiomatic U.S. English.
- Sound like a serious training partner giving a fast judgment: blunt, compact, and physically grounded.
- Prefer familiar Gym nouns and short declarative lines.
- Do not invent personal experience, user outcomes, or credentials.
- Avoid insults, humiliation, body shaming, and contempt for beginners.
- Avoid unsupported `best`, `worst`, `optimal`, `guaranteed`, `everyone`, `never`, and `always` claims.
- Keep every slide legible at phone size.

## Title and caption

- Provide a separate concise post title that restates the bounded decision.
- Use the caption to resolve the ranking into one practical principle and preserve the narrowest evidence limitation that does not fit on-screen.
- Add only a small set of specific strength-training hashtags.
- A save prompt may appear when it follows naturally from the utility; do not add a dedicated CTA slide by default.
- Do not mention or promote LIFT CODE during the current no-product prelaunch phase.
